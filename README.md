# 💫 About Me:
From a young age, I’ve been captivated by systems—how things connect, function, and evolve. That curiosity evolved into a passion for creating technology from the ground up. Today, I’m not just a developer; I’m a builder, architect, and lifelong student of how software can shape human potential.<br><br>I’m currently building applications using a modern stack: Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, PostgreSQL, and Prisma, with Go powering the backend for performance and concurrency. I’ve designed my architecture around cloud and edge computing, optimizing for speed, scalability, and the handling of massive datasets. I also incorporate AI models into both the frontend and backend—using gRPC for fast, efficient communication between services. These tools aren’t just trendy—they reflect my drive to stay ahead of the curve, to build with precision and vision.<br><br>But technology alone doesn’t define me—resilience does. I didn’t come from a traditional tech background. I learned to code by trial, error, and late nights, pushing past the setbacks of my early academic years. I built my own Linux server from scratch—physically and digitally—to deploy applications, run simulations, and experiment with high-performance computing. I taught myself how to containerize environments with Docker, optimize systems with NGINX, and orchestrate production-ready pipelines.<br><br>Now, as the founder of Notic Inc, I channel all of this experience into building software that empowers others. Our mission is to enhance human cognition through intuitive, AI-powered tools that live at the edge—accessible, fast, and capable.<br><br>This journey—this stack of tools, ideas, and battles—has shaped who I am. Without it, my application wouldn’t just be incomplete; it would be missing the very thing that makes me me: the drive to engineer the future, one system at a time.
